How to play

  1. Two players take turns playing.
  2. Click on a column to drop your disc.
  3. Discs fall to the lowest empty space.
  4. Connect four discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  5. If the board is full and no one wins, the game ends in a tie.
